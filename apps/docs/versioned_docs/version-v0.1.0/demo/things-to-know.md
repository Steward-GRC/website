# Things to know

## Simulated or limited

- **AI is simulated.** Generate, Improve, Review, Rewrite, summaries and Ask give made-up text. Do not judge the wording. Judge where AI fits in the flow.
- **No email is sent.** Notifications and sign-in codes do not leave the demo.
- **Data resets whenever the demo is updated.** Documents you create may disappear without warning. Do not put anything you want to keep in it.
- **Test data only.** Do not enter real or private information.
- **Uploads are limited.** Files and images are checked simply and may be refused for size or type. Allowed types are PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, image and video. The size limit is 10 MB, and also CSV, Markdown and RTF files are allowed.
- **Some screens say "Coming soon".** Those parts of Steward are not built yet.
- **Second-factor and passkey sign-in** are not part of the Dev quick login. Use the dropdown, as the guide says.

## Known rough edges

- The submit dialog says you cannot edit while in review, while a later message says you can keep editing. Tell us which you saw.
- Some admin pages may be reachable by one test person and not another. Tell us what Grace and Dave could open.

## Giving feedback

The apps have a **Feedback** link in the header, but its page says "Coming soon" for now. Until it works, tell the person who sent you the link. Useful details:

1. The page address.
2. Who you were signed in as.
3. What you clicked.
4. What you expected, and what happened.

Anything that confused you counts, including words you did not understand.
