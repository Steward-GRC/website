# Reading and history

Publish a document first (see [Approvals](./approvals.md)). Then read it as a reader.

## The reader page

<Screenshot slot="reading-and-history-1" alt="The reader page" />

1. The **On this page** outline. Click a heading to jump to it.
2. The document text.
3. The details block: **Status**, **Sensitivity**, **Owner**, **Version**, **Published** and **Last updated**.
4. The tabs: **View**, one for each kind of link, and **Changes**.
5. The **Actions** menu: **Print / Export** and **History & approvals**.
6. The appendices, listed at the end of the outline.

**Steps:**

1. Choose **Library**, then the document.
2. Read the text. Use **On this page** to move around.
3. Scroll to **Appendices**. Each appendix shows a "Not policy" label: it is supporting material.

Notices at the top tell you if a newer draft or a version in review exists, or if this version was superseded or withdrawn. A word count and read time show near the title. An **AI summary** toggle exists. It appears only on a published document.

## History and approvals

<Screenshot slot="reading-and-history-5" alt="History and approvals" />

1. **History & approvals**, opened from the **Actions** menu.
2. The versions, newest first, each with its status.
3. An event: what happened, and when.

**Steps:**

1. Open **Actions**.
2. Choose **History & approvals**.
3. Read the list: Created, Submitted for approval, each stage approved, Published, and so on, with who and when.

## Compare versions

You need at least two published versions. Publish once, then start editing the published document (**Start editing** on the document), change it, submit and approve it.

<Screenshot slot="reading-and-history-2" alt="The Changes tab" />

1. The **Changes** tab.
2. The two version pickers, from the older version to the newer.
3. The **Unified** and **Side by side** switch.
4. The legend: added text is marked new, removed text is marked old.
5. A bar such as "2 unchanged sections"; press it to show them.

**Steps:**

1. Choose the **Changes** tab.
2. Pick the two versions in the pickers, the older on the left and the newer on the right.
3. Switch the layout with **Side by side** or **Unified**.

**Summarise changes** is AI and simulated.

## Print preview

<Screenshot slot="reading-and-history-3" alt="The print dialog" />

1. The "Print this version" dialog. Open it with **Print / Export** in the **Actions** menu.
2. **Include version history page**.
3. **Print**.

**Steps:**

1. Open **Actions**, then **Print / Export**.
2. Tick **Include version history page** if you want who approved it and when.
3. Choose **Print** to open the print preview.

The printout has the document number, version and status in the header, "Printed" with the time and your name, and a reminder to check Steward for the current version. Sensitive documents are watermarked. Admins also have **Export PDF** under **Admin actions**.

## Acknowledgement (policies only)

Policies can ask people to confirm they have read them. Procedures never do.

<Screenshot slot="reading-and-history-4" alt="The acknowledgement card" />

1. The "Acknowledge this policy?" dialog. Open it with **Acknowledge** on the "Please review and confirm" card.
2. Your name, the version and the time. They are recorded against your name.
3. **Acknowledge**.

**Steps:**

1. Open a policy that needs acknowledgement. Or choose **To acknowledge** in the left navigation and then **Read & acknowledge**.
2. Choose **Acknowledge**, then confirm.
3. The card changes to "Acknowledged" with the date.

Whether a policy asks for acknowledgement depends on the category's acknowledgement trigger (**None**, **On publish** or **On change**) and audience. A category uses its own setting, or the nearest parent's.
