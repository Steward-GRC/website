# Definitions, references and contacts

A document can link to reusable items. Admins make the items once. Authors attach them to a document. The links belong to that version of the document and are approved with it.

:::note
An admin creates the items first.
:::

## 1. Create items (admin)

An admin opens the admin app.

<Screenshot slot="library-1" alt="The Definitions page in the admin app" />

1. The **New definition** dialog.
2. The **Category** chooser. Definitions belong to a category.
3. **Term**.
4. **Definition**.
5. **Create definition**.

- **Definitions**: **New definition**, with **Term**, **Definition** and **Category**. A term must be unique.
- **References & standards** (References): **New reference**, with **Label**, **Kind** (**Standard**, **Text** or **Link**), **Clause**, **Link** and **Reference text**.
- **Contact library**: **New contact block**, with **Label**, **Name**, **Role**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Department**, **Hours**, **Notes**. Email or phone is required.

Items can be archived and restored. An item in use cannot be deleted, only archived.

## 2. Attach them to a document (author)

An author opens a draft.

<Screenshot slot="library-2" alt="The links section on a draft" />

1. **Definitions**, with the dropdown and **Add**.
2. **Related**, with the **Policy** and **Relationship** dropdowns and **Add**.
3. **References**, with **Add a reference**.
4. **Contacts**, with **Add a contact**.
5. The remove (X) button, and on references and contacts the up and down arrows.

**Steps:**

1. Under the editor, find the sections **Definitions**, **Related**, **References** and **Contacts**.
2. Pick an item in the dropdown and choose **Add**.
3. Use the arrows to reorder references and contacts. Definitions are always alphabetical.
4. Remove an item with the X button.

For **Related**, pick another policy and a relationship such as "is related to", "supersedes", "depends on", "implements" or "references". The other policy shows the opposite relationship. You need a second document to do this.

You can change links while the version is a draft, in review or withdrawn. After that they are read-only ("These can't be changed now"). Linking never creates a new draft.

## 3. See them in the reader

Open the published document as any person. Each kind has its own tab: **Related**, **Definitions**, **References**, **Contacts**.

<Screenshot slot="library-3" alt="The reader tabs" />

1. The **Definitions** tab.
2. The **Related** tab.
3. The **References** tab.
4. The **Contacts** tab, with department, email and phone.

When nothing is attached a tab says, for example, "No contacts attached." Defined terms and links are also included when you print.
